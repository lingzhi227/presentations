Shapley-Guided Rollout Selection for Reinforcement Learning with Verifiable Rewards

Under review · ICLR 2027

RL with verifiable rewards improves LLM reasoning, but selecting a subset of rollouts to cut training cost risks losing useful learning signals. We introduce Shapley Selection Rollouts (SSL), which values each response by its average marginal contribution to recovering the full group's update direction, and show gains of 0.49–0.62 pp on math benchmarks (GSM8K, MATH500, AIME24/25) when selecting 16 of 64 responses per prompt.

View-Decomposed On-Policy Distillation for Robust Reasoning in Large Language Models

Under review · ICLR 2027

On-policy distillation supervises models on their own generated trajectories, but it can transfer prompt-sensitive behavior from the teacher. View-Decomposed On-Policy Distillation (VD-OPD) compares a frozen teacher's predictions under two prompt views of the same prefix, using their shared probability mass as both target and weight — yielding more accurate reasoning, less sensitive to prompt wording.

Learning Not to Forget How to Improve: Meta-Forgetting in Self-Evolving Agents

Under review · ICLR 2027

Self-evolving agents can lose the ability to generate effective self-updates even as task performance rises — a failure we term meta-forgetting. Improvement Function Replay (IFR) revisits frozen historical agent states to select strategy updates that maximize current gains while preserving past improvement ability.

CoRD: On-Policy Distillation via Correction Reconstruction

Under review · ICLR 2027

Supervising fewer tokens in on-policy distillation can distort the collective learning signal when corrections cancel each other out. Correction Reconstruction Distillation (CoRD) selects complementary token corrections in an output-head gradient representation and assigns signed weights to reconstruct the dense mean update, with theoretical approximation bounds.

Milestone-Aware On-Policy Distillation for Long-Horizon Reasoning

Under review · AAAI

Milestone-Aware On-Policy Distillation (MA-OPD) organizes verified teacher solutions into a frozen geometry of semantic milestones and applies the OPD loss only to the earliest region where student progress stalls — improving mean Avg@8 by 2.15 points over the strongest baseline across six mathematical-reasoning benchmarks.
